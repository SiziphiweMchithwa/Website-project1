About Us

Innovating Solutions, Elevating Success.

Riseonic Advisory is an enthusiastic black-owned and managed consulting practice by Mr. Ndiphiwe Madikiza, Mr. Sazi Matebese and Ms. Mbasa Figlan who together have more than 15 years’ combined experience. All three directors Mr. Ndiphiwe Madikiza, Mr. Sazi Matebese and Miss. Mbasakazi Figlan model an entrepreneurial spirit, opportunistic leadership style, and commitment to excellence to ensure the sustainable growth of the practice.

Riseonic focuses on providing innovative integrated professional advisory project- related services in mixed-use, commercial development, educational facilities, retail, residential, industrial, property development, public & private infrastructure development, agriculture, contract mining, mining infrastructure, mining rehabilitation, as well as project finance & capital raising. The practice aims to provide world-class services so that clients can understand tailored solutions to achieve their business objectives and provide quality, efficient, and professional services.

Our Values!

Why Trust Us? Discover Our Commitment to Excellence!

At Riseonic Advisory, our core values drive everything we do. We are committed to integrity, delivering exceptional service, and fostering innovation in all our projects. Our team believes in collaboration, ensuring that every client feels valued and understood. Together, we strive for excellence and sustainability in every initiative.

Reliability

We prioritize consistency and dependability in delivering high-quality Advisory Solutions.

Innovation:

We cultivate a culture of innovation, empowering our team to explore creative solutions and stay ahead of industry trends, delivering impactful advisory services.

Strategic Thinking

With a strategic vision, we utilize insightful approaches to provide innovative advisory services that anticipate and meet client needs.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every aspect of our work, from initial concept to the meticulous details of project completion.

Open Collaboration

We believe in the power of open collaboration. By fostering an environment where ideas flow freely and collaboration is valued, we harness the collective intelligence of our team to drive success and innovation in every project.

Nurturing Talent

Recognizing that our success is driven by our people, we are dedicated to nurturing talent within our firm. We invest in the continuous learning and professional development of our team members, fostering a dynamic and skilled workforce.

Intergrity

Upholding the highest ethical standards, we conduct our business with honesty, integrity, and a commitment to fairness.

Client-Centric Focus

Our clients are our priority. We maintain a client-centric focus by actively listening to their needs, understanding their goals, and tailoring our services to ensure their success. Client satisfaction is the measure of our success. These values form the foundation of our Professional Advisory Practice, guiding our actions and decisions to create lasting success for our clients and stakeholders
